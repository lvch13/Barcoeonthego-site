What is Barcoe On The Go?

A small family-run, LGBTQ+-owned company building useful, affordable apps, games and web tools.

Barcoe On The Go is the home for all of my passion projects. Here's where the name comes from, who's behind it, and where we hope it's going.

The name

Barcoe represents my family name. On The Go represents me and my constant desire to keep learning, helping others and building things. I'm always on the go.

Why it exists

I created Barcoe On The Go as the company behind all of my passion projects, starting with Flarewell, along with our BOTG Games and free web tools. I have many more ideas in the works. Some of them will take a little more time to build, but I promise they'll be useful.

A family team

I build a lot of these apps with my brother. We brainstorm ideas together, and he keeps me from running away with my thoughts. We make a good team and balance each other out, so I guess you could say we're a well-oiled, family-run business.

Where we're from

Barcoe On The Go is based in Wilmington, North Carolina, because that's where I live right now. I'm actually a British expat, born and raised in Gloucester, England. Barcoe On The Go is also proudly LGBTQ+-owned.

Built for community

I'm all about community, and I want to build things that help mine in any way I can. One day, I hope Barcoe On The Go:

  • offers jobs to local people
  • is known for helping others
  • funds community projects
  • supports people who are oppressed
  • inspires others to do the same

We have a road ahead of us before we get there, and with your help, I'd love to make Barcoe On The Go successful in all of it. Using our tools, sharing them with someone who needs them, and sending us your ideas all help more than you'd think.
