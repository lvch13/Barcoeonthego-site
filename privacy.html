BOTG Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Barcoe On The Go LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile games published by its games division, Barcoe On The Go Games (BOTG Games), including Dizzy Donuts and Pearl Hop, and on this website. Our Flarewell app has its own privacy policy, available at flarewell.org.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or photos in our games. Player names in our games are generated nicknames chosen in the app, not real names.

Game progress such as scores and settings is stored on your device.

Advertising

Our games are free and show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), how ads are viewed or tapped, and basic crash and performance data. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Some of our games are played by children. For those games, we configure ads to be treated as child-directed, which means personalized (interest-based) advertising is turned off and ad requests are handled in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

On iPhone, we will only use your device's advertising identifier for tracking if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with the advertising provider described above, as needed to show ads in our games.

This website

This website uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use it, such as which pages are viewed. Google may set cookies and collect information like your IP address, device and browser type to do this. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
