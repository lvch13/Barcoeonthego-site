Freehold QR is live: free QR codes, an expiry checker and printable signs

By Laura, Barcoe On The Go · November 26, 2026

Freehold QR, the first of our free web tools, is now live at freeholdqr.com. It makes QR codes you own outright: no sign-up, no watermark, no scan limits and no expiry date.

What's in it

  • QR codes for almost anything: links, Wi-Fi, contact cards, email, texts, phone calls, WhatsApp, map locations, calendar events and plain text.
  • Your own style: colors, dot and corner styles, round or square codes, and your logo in the middle.
  • Downloads that print well: PNG, SVG or JPG, up to 4096 pixels, with a transparent background if you want one.
  • A scan check that tests every code before you download it.
  • A QR code expiry checker for codes you already have.
  • Printable signs for Wi-Fi, menus, reviews and wedding photos.
  • A bulk generator and a scanner.
  • Plain-English guides on things like QR code sizes and why some codes stop working.
  • Spanish and French versions of the site.

It all runs in your browser, nothing you type is uploaded, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded. You can even add it to your phone's home screen like an app.

Why we made it

We got tired of seeing “free” QR codes stop working once a trial ran out. We wrote more about that in Why we built Freehold QR.

What's next

Freehold QR is the first of a set of free tools from Barcoe On The Go, all built the same way: free to use, with no accounts and nothing you type uploaded. We'll post here each time a new one launches.

Try it out, and tell us what you think at hello@barcoeonthego.org.
