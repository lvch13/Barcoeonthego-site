Flarewell

Understand your patterns.

Flarewell helps people living with flares track symptoms, treatments and daily life in one place, so patterns are easier to spot and easier to share with their doctor.

  • Flare and symptom logging with severity, pain and triggers
  • Medication, supplement and treatment tracking
  • Food, hydration and energy tracking on one health calendar
  • Weekly reviews, pattern insights and doctor-ready PDF reports
Visit flarewell.org

About us

Barcoe On The Go LLC is a small, family-owned and LGBTQ+-owned software company in Wilmington, North Carolina. We design, build and publish mobile apps for iOS and Android, and free tools for the web.

Our work spans three areas: Flarewell, our health-tracking app; Barcoe On The Go Games (BOTG Games), our division for casual mobile games; and our Free Tools, including Freehold QR and Penny Passport.
