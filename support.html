Dizzy Donuts

iPhone

How do I play?

Your candy spins around the donut on its own. Tap anywhere to switch direction. Grab the sweets and dodge the sour lemons. Tap the ? on the home screen to see the instructions again.

Where is my progress saved?

Levels, awards and your best Endless score are saved on your iPhone. There's no account. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

Can I change my candy name?

No. You pick your candy name once, and it stays the same so it's always yours.

An ad didn't load

Ads need an internet connection. If an ad can't load, the game carries on without it. For "Watch ad to continue", you only get the extra life when the ad plays to the end, so try again when you're back online.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Dizzy Donuts free. The ads are set up for children: they are general-audience ads and are not personalized.

Something's broken

Email us at the address above with what happened and which level you were on. A screenshot helps a lot.

Pearl Hop

iPhone & iPad

How do I play?

Tap the left or right side of the screen to switch lanes. Dodge the red dangers (jellyfish, crabs, pufferfish, fishing nets and sharks) and grab the pearls. The further you swim, the faster it gets, and you'll move from the Coral Reef to the Kelp Forest and the Deep Sea. Turn on Little Swimmer mode on the home screen for a slower game.

How do I get new swimmers and outfits?

Collect pearls while you swim, finish missions, and open the daily chest. Tap the arrows on the home screen to see every swimmer and unlock new ones with pearls. Outfits are in the Wardrobe: tap one to try it on, then buy it to keep it. Anything you buy works on every swimmer.

Where is my progress saved?

Pearls, swimmers, outfits, medals and stickers are saved on your device. There's no account. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

An ad didn't load

Ads need an internet connection. If no ad is available, Pearl Hop gives you the reward anyway, and you can always tap Go back on the loading screen. If you close an ad before the end, you go back without the reward.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Pearl Hop free, and you only see one when you choose to (to keep swimming, double your pearls or open a bonus chest). The ads are set up for children: they are general-audience ads and are not personalized.

Something's broken

Email us at the address above with what happened and which device you're using. A screenshot helps a lot.
